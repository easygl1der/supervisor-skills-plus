# Audience: advisor vs peer

Parameter: `audience: advisor` (default) or `peer`. It changes placement,
not the facts.

## Advisor

- Lead with conclusions, blockers, and decisions needed.
- One idea per slide; simple sufficient conditions on the main slides.
- Derivations, extra robustness tables, full proofs, and notation dumps
  go to `\appendix`. Hyperlink backup slides back to the main slide they
  support.
- Ask for a decision in a boxed question, not a narrative.

## Peer

- Derivations and experiment details stay in the main body when they are
  the point of the meeting.
- Still one idea per slide. The general theorem can appear after the
  simple sufficient condition, not instead of it.
- Replication and assumption-violation slides are in the main body for
  paper-talk.

## Shared

- Sentence titles. Title verb strength matches claim level (quality-gates.md).
- Fewer slides than minutes.
- Do not pack a slide because Beamer makes it easy (slide-rules.md).
