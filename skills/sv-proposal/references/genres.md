# Genres: section maps and length budgets

The call document or school template always wins over this file. Limits
below were checked on the stated date and change between calls; copy them
into the call sheet only after confirming them in the current call.

## 1. The six core elements

Every genre below covers the same six elements, under different headings
and in different proportions:

| Code | Element | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Research question(s) and aims | What exactly will be answered? |
| E2 | Motivation and literature gap | Why does it matter, and what is missing? |
| E3 | Method and feasibility | How, and why will it work in this setting? |
| E4 | Timeline and milestones | When, and how will progress be checked? |
| E5 | Expected contributions | What will exist at the end that does not now? |
| E6 | Risks and alternatives | What can fail, and what happens then? |

When a template has no heading for an element (E6 is the usual casualty),
put it inside the nearest section rather than drop it.

## 2. Thesis proposal (kaiti report)

Reader: supervisor plus a panel of about three faculty; the outcome is
permission to proceed. Each university publishes its own template and
review form; obtain both, because the review form lists what the panel
scores. [src: guide:https://pe.upc.edu.cn (graduate school kaiti notices, fetched 2026-10-16)] (candidate)

Common section map (Chinese headings as they usually appear):

| Section | Chinese heading | Elements | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Topic source, purpose and significance | 选题依据 / 课题来源及研究目的和意义 | E2 | Lead with the problem, not the history of the field |
| Literature review | 国内外研究现状及分析 | E2 | Analysis and critique, not a list; end on the gap |
| Research content and objectives | 研究内容与研究目标 / 拟解决的关键问题 | E1 | Number the research contents; one per chapter is a good default |
| Methods, technical route, feasibility | 研究方案、技术路线及可行性分析 | E3, E6 | Technical roadmap figure expected; expected difficulties and countermeasures often required |
| Innovation points | 创新点 / 特色与创新 | E5 | Each maps to one research content; no generic claims |
| Schedule | 进度安排 | E4 | Align with the school's mid-term review and pre-defense dates |
| Expected results | 预期成果 | E5 | Papers, software, datasets, thesis chapters |
| Conditions and resources | 已具备的条件 | E3 | Data access, compute, collaborators |
| References | 参考文献 | | School minimums are common |

Length: school-specific. Examples seen in published rules: a PhD body of
at least 12,000 characters with at least 50 references at one university;
per-section minimums (for example at least 800 characters for purpose and
significance at PhD level) at another. Treat these as examples, not
defaults. [src: guide:https://law.shmtu.edu.cn (kaiti rules, fetched 2026-10-16); guide:https://mst.sysu.edu.cn (kaiti template, fetched 2026-10-16)] (candidate)

Panel focus: a feasible scope for the remaining years, a literature review
that shows command of the area, and research contents that become thesis
chapters. A good default is three research contents, each a chapter and a
paper, independent in the sense of section 4 of `register-and-aims.md`.

## 3. PhD research proposal (application)

Reader: an admissions committee and a prospective supervisor. The
proposal tests whether the applicant can frame a question, knows the
nearest literature, and picks a method that fits; committees expect the
project to change after admission. Length is set by the program; when the
program is silent, ask rather than assume.

| Section | Elements | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Title and one-paragraph summary | E1 | The summary is the Heilmeier answers compressed |
| Background and gap | E2 | Five to fifteen well-chosen references beat a long list |
| Research questions or aims | E1 | Two or three, answerable in the program's duration |
| Methodology | E3 | Data, design, analysis; why this method for this question |
| Timeline | E4 | By year or half-year; milestones not activities |
| Expected contribution and fit | E5 | Why this group, this supervisor, this program |
| Risks (often a short paragraph) | E6 | Shows judgment; committees read it as maturity |
| References | | |

## 4. Research statement (job market, fellowship)

Reader: a hiring or fellowship committee, mostly outside the subfield.
The statement is a research agenda, not one project: past work as
evidence of ability (past tense, with outcomes), then two or three future
directions (future tense), each with a first concrete step and why the
applicant is placed to take it. Length is set by the call; when it is
silent, ask. The future directions follow the same aims logic as a grant:
independent, each with a first milestone.

## 5. NSFC General and Young Scientists programs

For the 2026 cycle, NSFC's own site describes a slimmer body for the
General Program (mianshang) and Young Scientists Fund category C: three
parts, rationale (立项依据: why the work and what its value is), research
content (the applicant's own logic; no preset sub-outline), and research
foundation (prior accumulation and conditions). Fixed headings for
research plan, technical route and methods are no longer imposed for
those two types; the body should in principle not exceed 30 pages.
Other program types may keep older outlines. Always download the year's
template from ISIS rather than reuse a saved file or a news paraphrase.
Verified 2026-10-17 against official NSFC pages (not wire-service reprints).
[src: guide:https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/3381/2821/99242.html@2026-10-17; guide:https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/3381/2824/99667.html@2026-10-17] (confirmed)
ISIS: https://grants.nsfc.gov.cn . The 2026 application-and-closing notice
says applicants must log in after 2026-01-15 and fill the latest form.

| Part (Chinese heading) | Elements | What to put there |
|---|---|---|
| Rationale (立项依据) | E2, E5 | Significance, state of the art with critique, the gap, and why now |
| Research content (研究内容) | E1, E3, E4, E6 | Objectives, key scientific questions, content, approach, feasibility, schedule, risks, in the applicant's own order |
| Research foundation (研究基础) | E3 | Preliminary results, prior publications, conditions, team |

Before 2026 the body followed a fixed outline (rationale; research
content, objectives and key scientific questions; plan and feasibility;
innovation; annual plan and expected results; foundation and conditions).
Departments still circulate those templates; check which one the current
call uses. The program's general requirement reads as: important
scientific significance, sufficient theoretical basis, novel academic
ideas, clear objectives, concrete content, feasible plan. Applicants also
choose a scientific-problem attribute and justify it, which the key
scientific question must match.
[src: guide:https://kyc.gdufe.edu.cn (General Program filling instructions, fetched 2026-10-16)] (candidate)

## 6. NSF Project Description

| Section | Elements | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Overview and objectives | E1, E2 | What, why, how, how success is known, benefits |
| Relation to the state of knowledge | E2 | Including the PI's work under other support |
| Research plan | E3, E4, E6 | Plan quality and a mechanism to assess success are scored |
| Broader Impacts (separately labeled section) | E5 | Required as its own heading |
| Results from Prior NSF Support | E3 | When applicable; capped within the description |

Example limits from PAPPG NSF 24-1 (do not treat as a frozen default):
Project Description including visuals may not exceed 15 pages unless the
solicitation or an authorized deviation says otherwise; Results from Prior
NSF Support are limited to five pages within that; no URLs in the body.
A separately labeled Intellectual Merit heading is not required, but the
criterion is still scored; Broader Impacts remains its own labeled
section. Verify every number against the current PAPPG and the
solicitation before writing the call sheet.
[src: guide:https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation@2026-10-17] (confirmed as PAPPG 24-1 example)

## 7. ERC Part B1 and B2

Part B1 holds the extended synopsis (assessed at step 1); Part B2 holds
the full scientific proposal with sections on the state of the art and
objectives and on methodology (assessed at step 2). Older Advanced Grant
calls used example limits such as a 5-page synopsis and 14 pages for B2
sections (a) and (b), references excluded. Those numbers are historical
examples only. Confirm structure and limits in the current Information
for Applicants for the exact call (Starting / Consolidator / Advanced
and year). Do not copy last year's page count from memory.
[src: guide:https://projektservice-mathematik.univie.ac.at/fileadmin/user_upload/p_projektservice_mathematik/PDFs/EU/ERC/2024/ERCat3_PreparingAdG_FFG_2024_Feb.pdf@2026-10-16] (candidate; 2024 AdG briefing, not the live call)

| Section | Elements | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Extended synopsis (B1) | E1, E2, E5 | Must stand alone; most applications are decided here |
| State of the art and objectives (B2 a) | E1, E2 | Objectives beyond the state of the art |
| Methodology (B2 b) | E3, E4, E6 | Risks and how they are handled; timescale and resources justified |

## 8. NIH Specific Aims and Research Strategy

A common Specific Aims layout (opening on the problem; known and missing;
long-term goal, objective and central hypothesis; two or three aims;
closing on outcomes and impact) is widely taught. Treat "one page" and
the Significance / Innovation / Approach split as examples until the
funding opportunity announcement (FOA) is read; mechanism and FOA win.
[src: guide:https://www.casrai.org/guides/nih-specific-aims-page@2026-10-16; guide:https://poorvucenter.yale.edu/media/621/download@2026-10-16] (candidate layout guides)

The simplified peer-review framework (Importance; Rigor and Feasibility;
Expertise and Resources) applies to most research project grants with due
dates on or after 2025-01-25; confirm on the current NIH page.
[src: guide:https://www.grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/peer-review/simplifying-review/framework@2026-10-17] (confirmed)

The specific-aims pattern also works as the first page of any proposal,
including a kaiti report summary.

## 9. Length budget by element

Starting shares of the body when the call gives no internal split; adjust
to the call and the panel. These are this skill's defaults, not funder
rules.

| Element | Thesis proposal | PhD application | Grant |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 question and aims | 10% | 15% | 10% (plus a one-page aims summary) |
| E2 motivation and gap | 30% | 25% | 20% |
| E3 method and feasibility | 35% | 35% | 45% |
| E4 timeline and milestones | 10% | 10% | 10% |
| E5 contributions | 5% | 10% | 5% |
| E6 risks and alternatives | 10% | 5% | 10% |
