# sv-litreview-map

Literature-map mode of the former `deep-research`. Load
`literature-map.md` and follow it; the citation protocol and hedge ladder
apply unchanged, Step 0 to Phase 2 of the survey do not.

See: ../sv-litreview/references/literature-map.md
See: ../sv-litreview/references/citation-protocol.md
See: ../sv-litreview/references/hedge-calibration.md

The map figure uses the kit in `sv-figure-explain` (auto-dag-elk template),
as `literature-map.md` describes.
