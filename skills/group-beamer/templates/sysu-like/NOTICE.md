# sysu-like

Own Beamer theme. Colours only, taken as facts: `sysugreen` RGB (0, 88, 38)
and `sysugreendark` RGB (5, 71, 25). Outer `split`, colour `whale`/`orchid`,
inner `rounded`.

Do not copy files from https://github.com/yxnchen/sysu-beamer-template
(repo LICENSE claims CC0; `sysu.sty` header says LPPL and/or GPL; bundled
logos are Sun Yat-sen University marks).

Place logos, if the presenter has a right to use them, in untracked
`logos/` (`sysu_logo.png`). They are gitignored.
